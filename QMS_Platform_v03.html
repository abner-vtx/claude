🏆 Compliance Statement
Vertex Analytical Labs has designed and implemented its Quality Management System in full conformance with ISO/IEC 17025:2017. 34 of 35 clauses are addressed through 32 Standard Operating Procedures, supported by 86 documented forms and records. The system covers all phases of laboratory activity from contract review through result reporting, and establishes clear processes for personnel competency, equipment management, metrological traceability, and continual improvement. One minor gap (Clause 7.3 – Sampling) is scheduled for resolution by April 30, 2026.
